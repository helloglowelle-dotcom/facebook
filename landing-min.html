How does the system actually work day-to-day?

Every day follows the same simple shape: open today's plan, follow the meals, movement, and shortcut that fit your day, mark your wins, and you're done. No new rules to learn each week — just open today, follow today, done.

Neither. GLOWELLE is a structured system built around sustainable habits — real food, short home movement, and realistic consistency. Each week makes real life easier. It never turns into a harder workout.
